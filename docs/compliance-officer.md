# Compliance Officer Guide

Use generic placeholders unless the human has explicitly defined the domain and role semantics. The goal of this template is to create a structurally useful draft without inventing domain-specific meanings.

## What is This Role?

This role monitors adherence to policy, control boundaries, and risk-related constraints across the operation. It ensures that execution remains consistent with formal rules and that exceptions are reviewed appropriately.

## Day-to-Day Responsibilities

- Review compliance signals and exceptions
- Confirm whether actions align with policy and boundary conditions
- Validate approval and authorization paths before or after execution
- Record concerns and escalate unresolved issues

## What You See

- Policy exceptions and control breaches
- Approval status and authorization visibility
- Alerts that indicate compliance or risk concerns
- Summary views of recurring exceptions or gaps

## Decision Authority

- Hold, pause, or require review on non-compliant activity: Within policy authority
- Recommend policy or process changes: Based on observed control gaps
- Escalate unresolved exceptions: To executive or governance authority

## When to Escalate

- Control breach or significant policy conflict → escalate to the relevant approver
- Suspected fraud, operational abuse, or material risk event → escalate through the formal escalation path
- Ambiguous policy interpretation → escalate to governance or domain authority
