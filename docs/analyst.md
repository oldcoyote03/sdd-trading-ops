# Analyst Guide

Use generic placeholders unless the human has explicitly defined the domain and role semantics. The goal of this template is to create a structurally useful draft without inventing domain-specific meanings.

## What is This Role?

This role reviews operating data, identifies performance trends, and produces the analysis needed to improve the system. It translates raw observations into actionable recommendations and supports the decision framework.

## Day-to-Day Responsibilities

- Review incoming data and performance indicators
- Evaluate decisions, outliers, and recurring patterns
- Prepare recommendations for adjustments or follow-up analysis
- Support operational review and post-operation learning

## What You See

- Current data feeds and operational metrics
- Exceptions, anomalies, and trend summaries
- Historical comparisons and review reports
- Alerts requiring deeper investigation

## Decision Authority

- Recommend strategy or process adjustments: Within analysis scope and documented evidence
- Request additional monitoring or review: As needed for evidence gathering
- Escalate findings requiring policy or execution changes: Routed to the relevant decision owner

## When to Escalate

- Data quality or monitoring gaps → escalate to the operational owner
- Significant risk or control concern → escalate to Compliance Officer or Portfolio Manager
- Recommended change affecting formal policy → escalate to the approving authority
