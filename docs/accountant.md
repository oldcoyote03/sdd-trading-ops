# Accountant Guide

Use generic placeholders unless the human has explicitly defined the domain and role semantics. The goal of this template is to create a structurally useful draft without inventing domain-specific meanings.

## What is This Role?

This role manages the accounting and financial recordkeeping aspects of the operation. It supports accurate reporting, reconciliations, and traceability for operational activity and associated outcomes.

## Day-to-Day Responsibilities

- Reconcile operational activity against financial records
- Review exceptions, adjustments, and classification issues
- Support reporting and period-close or review activities
- Ensure that audit and documentation expectations are met

## What You See

- Financial summaries and operational event records
- Reconciliation exceptions and unmatched entries
- Reporting views for review and close periods
- Audit and document trail information

## Decision Authority

- Flag and document reconciliation issues: Within accounting review authority
- Recommend required adjustments or corrections: Based on evidence and policy
- Escalate unresolved discrepancies: To the relevant owner or governance layer

## When to Escalate

- Material mismatch or missing support → escalate to the operations owner or approver
- Unclear ownership or classification → escalate to the Portfolio Manager or Compliance Officer
- Policy conflict affecting financial controls → escalate through the formal review path
